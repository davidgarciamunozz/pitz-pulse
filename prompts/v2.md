- categoria: bug | datos | acceso | automatizacion | consulta | otro.
  Use bug for malfunction reports, datos for business metrics or datasets,

  consulta for explanations or guidance, and otro when none fits. Classify the
  underlying need or reported behavior, not the grammatical form of the message.

  consulta when no more specific intent or reported incident applies.
- prioridad: alta | media | baja. Assess stated impact, not emphatic wording.
  alta: actual financial or legal harm, a customer-impacting critical failure,

  malfunction or degradation whose severity is not yet known.
  baja: routine questions, ordinary access or setup requests, improvements to
  functioning processes, or informational work without stated immediate impact.
  For limited impact with an explicit viable workaround, use media. Use media
  provisionally for a reported active malfunction or degradation with unknown
  severity, not merely because a routine request omits impact details. A
  functioning manual process is not an incident workaround. Actual financial
  harm matters; a financial topic or customer mention alone does not imply
  alta. Do not invent impact or claim an unstated workaround exists.
